About TethTechLabs

TethTechLabs is an independent app studio based in Japan. We design and develop iOS, Android, and web applications focused on learning, productivity, personal collections, and everyday digital tools.

Our Approach

Calm User Experience

Practical and calm user experiences for everyday use.

Mobile-first Design

Product design optimized for mobile and web applications.

Meaningful AI Use

AI is used only where it adds meaningful value.

Long-term Usability

We value maintainability and long-term usability.

Privacy Respect

We design with respect for user privacy.

What We Build

TethTechLabs builds applications that help users organize information, continue learning, manage personal interests, and make everyday routines easier.

Background

Our work is informed by practical insights from real-world business workflows, review processes, operations, and everyday problem-solving. The TethTechLabs Team focuses on building tools that are useful, reliable, and respectful of the people who use them.

Location

TethTechLabs is based in Tokyo, Japan. For inquiries, please contact info@tethtechlabs.com.
