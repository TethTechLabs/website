Apps

TethTechLabs develops mobile and web applications for learning, productivity, personal collections, and everyday digital tools.

Housing Loan Simulator

Japanese working title 住宅ローン試算
Simulation Web

A mortgage repayment simulator. Change loan amount, term, and rate to see monthly payments, principal versus interest, and how a rate increase would split. It does not offer loans, advice, or applications.

Home Sale Proceeds Simulator

Japanese working title 住宅売却の手取り試算
Simulation Web

Estimates cash left after selling a home: remaining loan, selling costs, and capital gains tax. No signup and no appraisal requests. It does not offer valuations, tax advice, or introductions to agents.

Personal Collection Management App

Japanese working title Myスマコレ
Collection Management In development

A personal collection management app designed to help users organize, display, and enjoy their favorite items.

Professional Certification Study App

Japanese working title 社会人資格アプリ
Learning Support In development

A study app for working adults preparing for professional certifications. It supports practice, review, progress tracking, and strengthening weaker areas.

Daily Audio Learning App

Japanese working title ミミアビ

A daily audio learning experience designed for short, focused listening sessions that fit naturally into everyday routines.

100-yen Shop Ideas App

Utility Tools Coming Soon
